Why use SHAP over built-in importance:
  - Built-in importance is a single number per feature, averaged
    over the whole training set, biased toward continuous/high-
    cardinality features, and has no sign (no direction).
  - SHAP gives one value per feature PER ROW, has a sign showing
    which direction the feature pushed that specific prediction,
    and the row's SHAP values sum exactly to (prediction - base
    value) - so it's an actual explanation, not just a ranking.

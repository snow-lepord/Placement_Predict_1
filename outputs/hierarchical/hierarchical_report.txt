Hierarchical vs K-Means, the core tradeoff:
  Hierarchical builds one tree and lets K be chosen after the fact
  by cutting at different heights - no refit needed per K. K-Means
  needs a full refit for every K tried (Session 21's K=2..10 sweep)
  but scales to far larger n, since it never needs the full pairwise
  distance matrix hierarchical clustering requires.
